About

Who the Gofrs are

“The Gofrs (pronounced Gophers) is a community-formed group working together to better the entire Go ecosystem.”

The group came together in the summer of 2018 to take over one of the most popular UUID packages in the Go ecosystem, github.com/satori/go.uuid, after it stopped being actively maintained. That work became gofrs/uuid, and the group has since looked at contributing to more projects.

Their efforts centre on picking up maintenance of projects that are widely used or have a large impact, and on considering new solutions to problems that arise as the number of Go programmers continues to grow.

A core part of the group’s purpose is lowering the barrier to contributing: making it easy for people to join the organization, and helping those newer to open source gain experience and learn to be positive open source citizens.
